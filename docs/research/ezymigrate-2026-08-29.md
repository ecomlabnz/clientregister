# EzyMigrate — competitor briefing

Research date: **29 August 2026**. All URLs fetched on that date unless a Wayback
timestamp is given.

**How to read this file.** Every factual claim carries its source URL in brackets on
the same line. `[their claim]` marks something EzyMigrate asserts that I could not
corroborate from any independent source. `[inference]` marks my own reasoning.
Quotes are verbatim from the page.

**One thing to know before anything else:** EzyMigrate's website is currently two
sites layered on top of each other. A **2026 rebuild** (homepage, `/about/`,
`/roadmap/`, `/how-it-works/`, and ~35 per-feature pages) sits alongside an
**un-retired 2022–23 layer** (`/the-ezymigrate-difference/`, `/faqs/`,
`/ezysolution-for-your-business-problems/`, `/visa-management/`, `/tracker/`,
`/immigration/`, `/awards/`). The two layers describe materially different products
and contradict each other repeatedly. Both are live, linked from the sitemap, and
indexed. Section 9 lists the contradictions.

`ezymigrate.co.nz` — the second domain in the brief — **is dead.** It returns
`Error 403 - This web app is stopped.` from Microsoft Azure App Service
[https://ezymigrate.co.nz/ and https://www.ezymigrate.co.nz/how-it-works.html, both
fetched 29 Aug 2026]. So `www.ezymigrate.co.nz/how-it-works.html` could only be read
from the Wayback Machine. The live application now lives at `app.ezymigrate.com`
[https://ezymigrate.com/ — "Login" link].

## 3. Pricing and packaging

**EzyMigrate does not publish pricing.** This is a firm finding, not a gap in my
research:

- `https://ezymigrate.com/pricing/` returns a genuine **HTTP 404**.
- There is **no pricing page in their sitemap**
  [https://ezymigrate.com/page-sitemap.xml — 74 URLs, none pricing-related].
- There is no "Pricing" item anywhere in the site navigation [https://ezymigrate.com/].
- Their own FAQ handles it this way: *"How much does it cost? — Pricing scales with
  your practice size — get in touch and we will recommend a plan that fits your team."*
  [https://ezymigrate.com/]

So: **per-user vs per-practice, currency, billing period, tiers, minimum term, and
whether a free trial exists are all unpublished.** There is no free trial offered
anywhere on the site — the only call to action is "Book a free demo"
[https://ezymigrate.com/demo/], described as a **group** demo running "Mon/Wed 11:00 –
12:00 · Tue/Thu 1:00 – 2:00" [https://ezymigrate.com/demo/]. The legacy FAQ describes
it instead as "a 30 minute virtual meeting" one-to-one [https://ezymigrate.com/faqs/] —
a minor contradiction.

### The two price anchors that do exist

**(a) Their own awards page — the only price figure EzyMigrate has ever published.**
For each of 2019, 2020 and 2021, the prize is described identically:

> "1-year FREE subscription to an **enterprise package worth $3,000** and complete
> **set-up costs valued at $2,000**." [https://ezymigrate.com/awards/]

Currency is not stated but the context is a New Zealand institute award, so NZD is the
natural reading [inference]. Read at face value this implies an enterprise tier at
roughly **$3,000 per year** with a **~$2,000 one-off setup/onboarding fee** — as at
2019–2021. Whether $3,000 is per user or per practice is not stated. Treat as a dated
anchor, not current pricing.

**(b) Third-party directory listing — low confidence.** Software Finder states:

> "Ezymigrate pricing starts at **$185/user/month** with a **one-time onboarding fee of
> $350**, specifically for VISA members. However, there are special discounts and
> concessions available for Victorian Immigration Solicitors Alliance (VISA) members."
> [https://softwarefinder.com/crm/ezymigrate]

I would not rely on this. The page is self-contradicting (the fee is simultaneously
"specifically for VISA members" and the general starting price), it names an Australian
body irrelevant to an NZ-first vendor, it states no currency, and the same page also
asserts "Ezymigrate does not offer an API" and "Ezymigrate does offer a dedicated
mobile app" — the latter contradicting EzyMigrate's own pages (§4b). Software Finder
carries **zero reviews** for the product ("Be the first one to leave a review!"), which
is consistent with an auto-generated listing. Record it as *a number that is circulating
in the market*, not as EzyMigrate's price. Note it has already propagated: a competitor's
comparison blog repeats "around $185 per user per month with a one-off onboarding fee"
[https://app.lodgehq.com.au/blog/best-crm-for-migration-agents-australia-2026].

**Techjockey** lists the product as **"Price On Request"**
[https://www.techjockey.com/detail/ezymigrate].

### Packaging signals

- The word "enterprise package" [https://ezymigrate.com/awards/] implies **named tiers**
  exist, but no tier list is published anywhere.
- Onboarding/migration is a paid, human-delivered service: "Our onboarding team migrates
  your clients, cases and documents for you — usually within a few days."
  [https://ezymigrate.com/] — cost not stated; the awards page valued "complete set-up
  costs" at $2,000 [https://ezymigrate.com/awards/].
- They also sell **agency services** alongside the software: "Adviser websites",
  "Landing pages", "Google Ads" ("Managed campaigns on high-intent visa keywords"),
  "SEO & AEO" — described as "Productised, fixed-scope, fast turnaround"
  [https://ezymigrate.com/roadmap/]. No prices.
- A "design partner" programme is offered with **"lifetime pricing"** as the incentive
  [https://ezymigrate.com/roadmap/] — which implies they expect list price to rise
  [inference].
- Paid training is implied but not priced: "EzyMigrate CRM Training — Live group
  training — 4 sessions a week" and "EzyAI Setup & Training"
  [https://ezymigrate.com/trainings/].

## 4. The specifics

### 4a. Ezyform / form filling

**This is the most important finding in the file, and the two site layers give opposite
answers.**

**What ships today (legacy pages, present tense):** a form filler that produces a
document a human then prints, signs and lodges. The claim is stated identically in a
footer banner repeated across every legacy page:

> **"Now You Can Fill in all Your Visa Application Forms Online — Save time by filling
> in all your visa application forms digitally and then printing them."**
> [https://ezymigrate.com/faqs/, https://ezymigrate.com/awards/,
> https://ezymigrate.com/ezysolution-for-your-business-problems/,
> https://ezymigrate.com/about-the-founder/]

And on the archived `.co.nz` marketing site, the same feature: *"Online application
forms — Save time by filling all the Visa application forms online and then print, sign
– and you're done."* [Wayback 4 Feb 2026,
https://web.archive.org/web/20260204084910/https://www.ezymigrate.co.nz/how-it-works.html]

The mechanism, such as it is described: *"With Ezyform, you'll be able to extract
information stored in Ezymigrate and use it to automatically fill out visa application
forms."* [https://ezymigrate.com/ezysolution-for-your-business-problems/] and *"With
Ezyform, you can also transfer data from Ezymigrate to any digital form"*
[https://ezymigrate.com/how-we-can-transform-your-business/].

**So: a completed PDF that a human lodges. Not electronic submission.** [inference, but
a well-supported one — "print, sign – and you're done" is unambiguous.]

**Which INZ forms:** *never specified anywhere.* No form number (INZ 1000, 1015, 1017,
1178, 1225…), no form count, no list, no screenshot of a form. The dedicated blog post
on the subject runs ~900 words and names not one form
[https://ezymigrate.com/the-automated-form-filling-software-which-is-boosting-the-immigration-consultancy-industry/].
The `/forms/` page — titled "Immigration Forms Software" — **has no body content at
all**, only a blog sidebar [https://ezymigrate.com/forms/].

**Official INZ integration: no, and they say so themselves.** The roadmap states
plainly:

> **"INZ and Immigration Australia don't offer APIs. So we built around that — a
> vision-based agent that reads Ezymigrate, navigates the adviser's authenticated
> portal session and fills forms field by field. The adviser reviews and submits.
> Always."** [https://ezymigrate.com/roadmap/]

There is **no mention anywhere on the site** of Immigration Online, ADEPT, an INZ API,
or any INZ system integration — I swept the full text of all 68 pages I crawled for
"ADEPT", "Immigration Online" and "API": zero hits. The closest thing claimed is manual
document upload via linked cloud storage: *"Users can select documents right from
Ezymigrate's cloud-based drives and upload them directly to online immigration
services"* [https://ezymigrate.com/ezysolution-for-your-business-problems/] and *"Our
linked Cloud-Based Drives help you submit relevant documents to Immigration NZ's online
services"* [https://ezymigrate.com/how-we-can-transform-your-business/]. That is a file
picker, not an integration. [inference]

**What is on the roadmap:** the AI form-filler is explicitly unbuilt. Its own page reads
in full:

> **"Coming soon · On our 2026 roadmap — Form Filling — A vision-based agent that fills
> government visa forms field-by-field in your authenticated portal session — you review
> and submit. Always."** [https://ezymigrate.com/form-filling/]

The nav tags it `Soon` [https://ezymigrate.com/]. The roadmap dates it to **"Month 9"**
of a 6–9 month plan and scopes it to **"the 5 highest-volume NZ visa categories"**
[https://ezymigrate.com/roadmap/]. The described flow is five steps: "Trigger from case
→ Pre-flight check → Fill in browser → Adviser reviews → Adviser submits", with "The
agent never clicks submit" [https://ezymigrate.com/roadmap/].

**How long "soon" has meant soon.** An EzyMigrate blog post dated **23 August 2021**
(author Adam Hyatt) answers the question *"When is the Mobile App and Ezyform going to
be available?"* with *"We are so excited about these game-changing features! We want to
ensure they are perfect for you! Our goal is for them to be completed during the final
stage 4 of the Release Plan."* [Wayback 10 May 2026,
https://web.archive.org/web/20260510120359/https://ezymigrate.com/ezymigrate-release-plan/].
That is **five years ago**, and form filling is still tagged `Soon`. This is the single
strongest piece of evidence in the file about their delivery record. [inference from
two dated primary sources]

**It exists** — the roadmap lists "Client portal" under "Already built"
[https://ezymigrate.com/roadmap/] — but the 2026 site has **no client-portal feature

  directly" [https://ezymigrate.com/ezysolution-for-your-business-problems/]; "clients

  [https://ezymigrate.com/the-automated-form-filling-software-which-is-boosting-the-immigration-consultancy-industry/].
- **Fill in forms** — "They'll even be able to fill out forms, saving you valuable admin
  time." [https://ezymigrate.com/ezysolution-for-your-business-problems/]
- **See case status** — "clients can see for themselves where their case is at"
  [https://ezymigrate.com/ezysolution-for-your-business-problems/]; the portal is

- **Message the adviser** — "Ezymigrate's Chat feature lets clients communicate directly

  [https://ezymigrate.com/visa-management/].
